## Contract and work

The new `internal/trash` package is a durable coordination boundary over the
existing storage and normalized ports. It stores an exact per-entry manifest,
flattened item rows, retention and client association evidence, and a janitor
record for each purge or restore operation. It never calls upstream APIs,
filesystem paths, or shell commands directly.

Completed behavior:

- default 30-day UTC retention and per-request retention bounded to ten years;
  expiry is recorded only after the trash effect is accepted, and early janitor
  work is skipped;
- exact root-relative manifest validation, child expansion, duplicate/path
  overlap checks, identity and size preflight, byte and item bounds, and
  immutable request/idempotency digests;
- whole-download stop observation before trash and again before every purge
  payload mutation; a missing download record is already-satisfied metadata
  state, while an unobservable or resumed client holds the operation;
- restore/purge mutual exclusion through SQLite CAS plus janitor lease claims,
  startup/periodic lease recovery, destination collision checks, source identity
  checks, per-item state/effect evidence, and no re-add or resume on restore;
- metadata removal is a distinct post-payload purge effect and is represented
  without a delete-files fallback; unselected client payload is outside the
  stored manifest and cannot be selected by this service;
- partial, uncertain, cancelled, missing, changed-identity, and malformed
  effects remain held/reconcilable rather than becoming successful deletes;
  explicit `Retry` requires a fresh read-only reconciliation;
- explicit `PurgeRequest.HardDelete`/`Force` bypasses retention only for an
  existing stored trash entry and never broadens the persisted scope;
- bounded `Tick`/`Janitor`, `Recover`, `Reconcile`, and `Retry` methods provide
  periodic and startup recovery without a second queue or outbox.
